Institutional-grade refinements for high-turnover, fat-tail hunting strategies in volatile crypto-asset markets.

01

Turnover Optimization

L1: Objective Function
Current State
Daily turnover of 294%; signals drive immediate weight rebalancing.
Proposed Technique
L1-Regularization penalty on weight changes. Add λ ||wt - wt-1|1 to the cost function.
Expected Outcome
Suppresses "jitter" trades; only executes rebalancing when alpha outweighs the transaction cost penalty.
L2: Signal Processing
Current State
Raw signals lead to high-frequency flips in position.
Proposed Technique
Temporal smoothing via Exponential Moving Average (EMA) of the signal vector.
Expected Outcome
Filters high-frequency noise; converts instantaneous signals into persistent regimes.
L3: Execution Logic
Current State
Any change in signal triggers a trade.
Proposed Technique
Hysteresis Banding: Trade only if |Snew - Sold| > τ.
Expected Outcome
Eliminates small, costly adjustments that do not meaningfully change the portfolio's risk profile.
02

Hit Rate & Stability

L1: Validation
Current State
Win rate ~51%; high reliance on extreme positive outliers.
Proposed Technique
Meta-Labeling: Train a secondary Random Forest/XGBoost model to predict the probability of the primary signal succeeding.
Expected Outcome
Filters out low-probability trades, increasing the hit rate by reducing "false positive" entries.
L2: Market Context
Current State
Single logic applied regardless of market phase.
Proposed Technique
Hidden Markov Model (HMM) for Regime Detection (Trending vs. Mean-Reverting).
Expected Outcome
Prevents "fat-tail" hunters from trading in choppy, low-volatility ranges where they typically bleed.
L3: Signal Filtering
Current State
Signals triggered in low-liquidity/low-vol environments.
Proposed Technique
Volume-Weighted Volatility Filter: Suppress signals if current vol is below the 20th percentile of the rolling 30-day window.
Expected Outcome
Ensures the strategy only deploys when the "tail" energy required for profitability is present.
03

Risk Scaling & Leverage

L1: Volatility Targeting
Current State
Leverage tied exclusively to BTC volatility.
Proposed Technique
Portfolio Volatility Targeting: Scale leverage based on the realized vol of the entire portfolio σp.
Expected Outcome
Protects against idiosyncratic shocks in altcoins that BTC vol fails to capture.
L2: Adaptive Thresholds
Current State
Static volatility switches for leverage changes.
Proposed Technique
Dynamic Percentile Thresholds: Switch leverage based on the rolling 90-day volatility percentile.
Expected Outcome
Adjusts the "definition" of high/low volatility to the current market epoch.
L3: Asset Allocation
Current State
Leverage applied uniformly across positions.
Proposed Technique
Multi-Asset Risk Parity: Allocate weights wi ∝ 1 / σi.
Expected Outcome
Equalizes the risk contribution of each asset, preventing a single volatile altcoin from dominating the PnL.
