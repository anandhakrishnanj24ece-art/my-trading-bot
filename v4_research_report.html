Quantitative Enhancements
Linear Vol Targeting → Convexity-Adjusted Exposure

To solve the 'alpha gap', v4 implements Conditional Volatility Targeting. Instead of a rigid $1/\sigma$ scale, we introduce a Volatility-Expansion factor that aggressively increases leverage during low-volatility regimes that typically precede momentum breakouts.

w_t = \frac{\sigma_{target}}{\hat{\sigma}_t} \cdot \left( 1 + \phi \cdot \mathbb{1}_{\{\hat{\sigma}_t < Q_{20}\}} \right)

Where $\phi$ is the expansion coefficient and $Q_{20}$ is the 20th percentile of historical volatility.

Expected: Increased capture of early-trend acceleration.
Linear Probability → Non-Linear Conviction (DeepConviction)

Replacing linear scaling with a Sigmoid Conviction Curve. This eliminates 'coin-flip' trades near 50% confidence and exponentially scales position size as conviction enters the 'high-certainty' zone.

S(p) = m_{max} \cdot \left( \frac{2}{1 + e^{-\alpha(p - 0.5)}} - 1 \right)

Where $\alpha$ controls the steepness of the conviction transition.

Expected: Noise reduction & high-conviction amplification.
Equal Asset Weighting → Dynamic Correlation Clustering

Implementation of Dynamic Modularity-Spectral Clustering. Assets are grouped by real-time correlation. We apply a 'Diversification Boost' when assets are uncorrelated and aggressive deleveraging during Correlation Spikes.

L_{adj} = L_{base} \cdot \exp\left( -\lambda \cdot \bar{\rho}_{portfolio} \right)

Where $\bar{\rho}_{portfolio}$ is the average pairwise correlation of active assets.

Expected: Systemic risk mitigation & variance optimization.
Single-Horizon Signal → Multi-Horizon Anchor Integration

Integrating a long-term Anchor Signal to filter short-term noise. Trade execution is only permitted when the short-term alpha aligns with the macro-trend anchor, preventing over-trading in choppy markets.

\text{Signal}_{final} = \text{Signal}_{short} \cdot \text{sgn}(\text{Signal}_{anchor}) \cdot \text{IC}_{weight}
Expected: Higher win rate & reduced turnover.
