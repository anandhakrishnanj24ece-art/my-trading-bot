Adaptive Weighted Strategy v3

Optimizing return capture and responsiveness while maintaining the risk floor of Pro v2.

1. Dynamic Lambda Tuning (SNR)

Current State (v2)

Static L1 regularization or simple momentum-based scaling of $\lambda$. Leads to over-sparsity in high-conviction regimes and noise-capture in low-SNR regimes.

Proposed v3 Technique

Implementation of Square-root Lasso (SQL). By making $\lambda$ pivotal, we decouple the penalty from the noise level $\sigma$, allowing for adaptive sparsity based on signal strength.

Implementation Formula
Minimize: 1/sqrt(n) * ||Y - Xβ||₂ + λ||β||₁
Optimal λ ≈ sqrt(log(p)/n)

The Square-root Lasso is preferred over Adaptive Lasso as it is pivotal, meaning the optimal $\lambda$ is independent of $\sigma$, removing the need for an external noise estimator that often lags in volatility spikes.

Expected: Reduced signal lag & tighter sparsity control

2. Non-Linear Hysteresis (The Elastic Band)

Current State (v2)

Flat thresholds for regime entry/exit. This creates a "dead zone" that is too wide during high volatility (missing turns) and too narrow during low volatility (whipsawing).

Proposed v3 Technique

Volatility-Adaptive Gating. The hysteresis band $\tau$ is now a function of realized volatility $\sigma$, shrinking during high-vol regimes to force faster responsiveness.

Implementation Formula
Upper Threshold (rU) = kU * σt
Lower Threshold (rL) = kL * σt
Hysteresis Band Δτ = (kU - kL) * σt

By scaling the buffer zone by $\sigma_t$, the strategy effectively "tightens" its requirements for a regime shift when the market is moving fast, eliminating the response lag observed in v2.

Expected: Higher turnover in vol-spikes / lower drawdowns

3. Advanced Risk Decoupling

Current State (v2)

Linear Vol Targeting. Exposure is scaled strictly by $1/\sigma$. This is overly conservative in "Low Vol / High Momentum" phases where risk-adjusted returns are highest.

Proposed v3 Technique

Equal Risk Contribution (ERC) + Convexity Scaling. Shift from simple vol-targeting to a risk-budgeting approach that accounts for tail-risk (CVaR).

Implementation Formula
Risk Contribution (RCi) = wi * (Σw)i / σp
Leverage Scaling (L) = Target_Vol / σp * f(Convexity)

We introduce a non-linear scaling factor $f(\text{Convexity})$ that allows leverage to expand slightly during high-momentum/low-vol regimes, while aggressively curtailing it using Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR) during tail-events.

Expected: Increased return in "Goldilocks" regimes

4. Meta-Labeling v2 (Prob-Weighted)

Current State (v2)

Binary filter (Trade/No-Trade). This "all-or-nothing" approach discards marginal alpha and creates jumpy equity curves.

Proposed v3 Technique

Probability-Weighted Exposure. Use a calibrated meta-model to output $P(\text{Success})$ and linearly scale the position size.

Implementation Formula
Exposure = Min_Risk + [(P(success) - Threshold) / (1 - Threshold)] * (Max_Risk - Min_Risk)

This bridges the gap between Conservative and Aggressive settings. Trades with $P \approx 0.6$ receive minimal sizing, while $P \approx 0.9$ trades receive full allocation, smoothing the equity curve and increasing the capture rate.

Expected: Higher Sharpe via optimized capital allocation
